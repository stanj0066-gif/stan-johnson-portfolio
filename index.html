About

My career started in stucco, shop fabrication, and painting, and grew into commercial electrical work, structural steel erection, welding, concrete, and excavation across Utah, Colorado, Washington, Texas, Ohio, and Missouri. Along the way I learned to run a 3D robotic plasma cutting arm, install insulated metal panels, and keep a fabrication shop's weld operation supplied and moving.

Working across that many trades taught me how a project comes together from start to finish. It also taught me to adapt quickly, work safely, and hold myself to a job-site standard of quality.

Today I'm bringing that experience into my own landscaping business, which I'm setting up as an LLC while taking freelance jobs and working in excavation. I'm also building my skills through the Tech-Moms development course.
